# WELCOME TO THE FORESIGHT TEAM

Welcome to the github page of Team Foresight!

## ABOUT US
We're a group of friends who make games. 
Our first game will be `Broken Bonds`, which will be a visual novel.

We formed in 2023 when Nathan had the idea to make a visual novel.
He brought some friends on board, and here we are.

## STAFF

Nathan is the director of the team. He's the primary writer and programmer.
He's also assists in composing the soundtracks.
He started out as a python programmer, but has since specialised in C#.

### Jess: Writer, editor, proofreader
Jess is predominantly a writer/editor, but also provides backup for proofreading.

### JC: Soundtrack, editor, proofreader
